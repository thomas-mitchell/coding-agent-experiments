Dave has been on this floor since it opened

The gym started in a brick warehouse on Ferrier Street in 2009 with four racks and a borrowed forklift. The floor has grown since. The approach hasn’t: learn the lifts properly, add weight slowly, keep turning up.

Every new member gets an hour on a platform before they get a fob. That hour covers the squat, the bench and the deadlift, what a warm-up set is actually for, and how to bail safely when a rep doesn’t go. It’s free, and nobody is sold anything at the end of it.

Four coaches work the floor across the staffed hours. If your setup looks off, one of them will tell you — that’s the job, and it’s why this place is quiet on injuries.
