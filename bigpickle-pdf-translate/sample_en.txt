The evolution of artificial intelligence and natural language processing.

(NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence that allows computers to understand, interpret, and generate human languages. In recent years, the development of deep learning has dramatically increased the accuracy of machine translation.

In particular, the appearance of transform models greatly improved the ease and context of translation. Today many systems can be run at high speed in local environments.

Chapter 2: Practical Applications

Machine translation technology is used in a wide range of fields, such as translating multilingual documents, smoothing international business, sharing academic research. Local model translation is the safest option in an environment where privacy is important.

By running a local translation, you can safely translate sensitive documents even when you have no Internet connection. This helps us avoid the risk of a data leak.
